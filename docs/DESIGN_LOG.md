## 001 - Do not choose the score before testing it

**Status:** agreed for now

Will test at least three scoring methods before picking one. There is no known best score for free-form LLM answers, and choosing one too early would only give us a nicer-looking assumption.

Will revisit this after we have labels, results, error examples, and timing data.
